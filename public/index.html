    Performance & Competition Teams

    Ready for the big stage?

    Our teams compete across the Northeast — clogging, jazz, hip hop, acro, lyrical, contemporary and musical theatre. Routines are taught during regular class time, so your dancer gets stage-ready without a second schedule.

    5+Clogging, Jazz, Hip Hop & Acro teams
    7+Musical Theatre team
    9+Contemporary team
    What being on a team means
    • Team dancers also take a technique class — Ballet, Jazz, Modern or Contemporary (Hip Hop counts for Clogging teams).
    • Extra rehearsals before competitions are mandatory so every routine is polished.
    • Competition fees are separate from tuition and due in February — details come as dates approach.
    • After one full team season, dancers can earn a solo, duo or trio with great attendance and an account in good standing.

    Box Office

    Simple monthly tuition.

    Admit one class$40–45per class, per month
    All-access pass$160unlimited classes for one dancer, per month
    Family discount10% offmonthly tuition when your family takes 3+ classes

    Due by the 8th of each month (a $10 late fee applies after). Tuition is the same every month regardless of holidays, vacations or absences.

    Registration fee once a year, with a family rate when two or more family members register. Costume fee per class — the first at registration, the rest by December (non-refundable after Dec 1).

    Pay by cash, check, Venmo @Steppin-Out-Dance, or autopay through the Parent Portal.

    Parent Portal

    Open the Parent Portal ↗
    First time? How to set up your account
    • Click “Create Account” and enter the parent’s name, email and phone.
    • Add each student — if they’re already in our system they’ll link automatically.
    • Accept the waiver, then choose “Register for Classes,” pick Recreational or Performance Team, and submit.
    • We’ll review it and email your monthly tuition and payment options.

      Dress Code

      Ballet, Lyrical & Pointe
      Black leotard, pink tights, skirt or fitted shorts, hair in a bun.
      Jazz, Contemporary, Acro & Combo
      Leotard & tights, fitted tops, dance shorts, pants or leggings.
      Clogging, Hip Hop, Irish & Adult
      Dance pants or shorts with a tee or tank.

      Hair secured off the face and proper shoes for every class. Our in-studio shop can size your dancer and special-order anything we don’t stock.
